# Code of conduct

## Our standards

Contributors must help keep this project welcoming and professional. Treat other people with respect, accept constructive feedback, and focus disagreements on the work.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may remove comments, commits, issues, code, or other contributions that violate these standards. They may also temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Maintainers will review reports fairly and keep details private where possible.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone is publicly representing the project.
